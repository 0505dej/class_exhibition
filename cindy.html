Description

The original artwork is Composition with Red, Blue, Black, Yellow, and Gray by Piet Mondrian, made in 1921. It uses straight black lines and blocks of color. The shapes have different sizes, but they feel balanced together. I chose this painting because I like how simple shapes and colors can create a strong composition.
