Inspired Artwork

Description

This website explores the idea of simplicity, reducing something complex to its most essential form.
Just as Matisse transformed the form of a snail into simple shapes and colors, the website will gradually simplify familiar objects through interaction.
As users move through the experience, details disappear, forms become abstract, and only the essential remains.
How much can we remove while still keeping what matters?
